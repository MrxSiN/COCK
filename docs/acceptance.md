The mechanical host may copy exact bytes, materialize literal fixtures, run installed tools and independently veto output. It cannot supply app-specific logic, infer success from a path or replace missing BF orchestration. A provisional zero status alone does not prove physical closure.

Six callback regressions check successive IDs, sink errors, an owned guard, exhausted IDs, maximum-ID wrap protection and forced store-exclusive failure. Forced failure must terminate after sixteen attempts with guard zero, no guest effects and status 14. Independent BF comparisons check complete records.

Stage B begins only after Stage A acceptance. Later-stage documentary checklists remain designs until executable source, tests and target evidence exist.
